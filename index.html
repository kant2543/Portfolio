Great decisions are not made on instinct alone — they are built on clean data, clear thinking, and the courage to act on both.

My career started in the field at Charoen Pokphand Group: running a provincial sales team, launching new products, and driving modern-trade partnerships. That ground-level experience is what shapes how I build tools today.

At C.J. Express, I moved from multi-province field operations into HQ operations strategy — managing sales targets, branch analytics and competitor intelligence for a network of around 2,300 stores, and building the internal systems behind them.

I work hands-on across the stack: BigQuery and SQL for pipelines and alerting, Google Apps Script and vanilla JavaScript for field-facing web apps, Leaflet for geospatial analysis, and LINE OA plus LLMs for automation that reaches people where they already work.
